Reclaim – Media & Photo Recovery: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Kervoxa · Effective: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Reclaim - Media & Photo Recovery (package com.reclaim.media), published by Kervoxa, handles information.

Summary

Reclaim works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, remote crash reporting or media upload service. It does not request Internet access, does not collect personal data on any server, and does not sell or share personal information.

Information processed on your device

With your permission, Reclaim processes photos and videos you authorize, along with their file names, sizes, dates, readable metadata/EXIF, scan results and operation history. This processing happens locally on your device and is never transmitted to Kervoxa.

Permissions

Android controls photo/video library permissions and individual file or folder grants. You may decline or revoke them at any time in Android Settings. If you select a cloud-backed provider, that provider may use its own network connection under its own policy; reading such a selection is not an upload by Reclaim.

Protected copies and retention

Verified protected copies, source references, hashes and related records are kept in private app storage on your device. You control copy deletion, storage quotas and optional retention. Background checks depend on Android scheduling and your folder grants. Uninstalling Reclaim or clearing its storage removes private protected copies and local records. Reclaim's app data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

History, exports and metadata edits

Clearing scan and operation history keeps protected and exported media. Copies exported to the shared library remain until you or another authorized app deletes them. Metadata edits create new verified copies and leave originals unchanged. Temporary working files are removed on completion or cancellation.

Sharing

Files are shared only when you explicitly choose a destination app in Android's share sheet. The receiving app handles the file under its own policy. Kervoxa does not automatically transmit media, inventories or EXIF data to anyone.

Deletion

Safe Delete saves a verified private copy before asking Android to move a source to Trash. Deletion without Trash asks Android to remove a specific item and does not create a backup. Neither feature promises forensic erasure, removal of other copies, or recovery of permanently erased internal-storage data.

Accounts and data deletion

Reclaim has no user accounts, so no account deletion is needed. All local data can be removed inside the app or through Android Settings (Clear storage or Uninstall).

Children

Reclaim is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Kervoxa will update this page if the app's data practices change, and will revise the effective date above.
